# Brag Plan: LifeOS (hype trailer)

## What is this app?
LifeOS is a personal life operating system — Now, School, Tasks, Calendar, Notes, Focus, plus optional Work / Abroad / Treasury / Mastery — one workspace instead of a pile of tabs.

## The angle
Not a feature resume. A trailer — but every claim is product-true. Problem slam → name reveal → real UI doing real things → environments/packs that actually exist → logo payoff. Hype energy, no invented features.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
Full-bleed dark. Giant type: **YOUR LIFE WAS SPLIT ACROSS APPS.** Hard zoom. Cut.

## Key moments (the middle)
- **Name slam** — LifeOS mark + **YOUR LIFE, IN FOCUS.**
- **Capture stunt** — Now command bar types `/t Finish reading chapter 3`; task row impacts in
- **Environment barrage** — Life · School · Work · Study Abroad · Treasury · Mastery slam one by one
- **School punch** — class cards + due row + GPA under **Classes, coursework, and grades.**
- **Focus drop** — Focus mode with timer; **Enter Focus.** + “Timer, checklist, and notes stay with you.”
- **Optional packs** — Work · Abroad · Treasury · Mastery with accurate one-liners

## Outro / punchline
Mark scales in. **LIFEOS.** Then **ONE OS. EVERY PART OF LIFE.** Cut to black on the hit.

## User flow worth showing
Capture → School/Tasks reality → Focus — framed as trailer beats, not a walkthrough.

## Tone
- Preset: cinematic (with chaotic pacing in the mid-barrage)
- Creative direction: overproduced personal-OS launch trailer
- Interpretation: Big type, hard/zoom cuts, short holds that still read, music louder, impact SFX on every slam. UI is the proof after the claim.

## Format: landscape — 1920x1080
## Duration: 24 seconds

## Visual identity (from the project)
- Dark stages `#0a0a0c` / `#17171b` for stingers
- Product paper `#F6F7F9` for UI proof
- Accent `#625AF6` / `#8B83FF` as energy, not a purple wash
- Env colors for chips
- Display: EB Garamond for product serif moments; Inter Black/Bold for ALL-CAPS stingers
- Brand-mark equalizer bars as signature slam element

## Share copy (draft)
Stop running your life in tabs. LifeOS — your life, in focus. Capture, school, calendar, focus, and the packs you turn on when it gets real.

## Audio direction
- Role: dense rhythmic / cinematic support
- Music: vol-1 bed at higher presence (~0.28–0.32), hard start
- Music treatment: punchy from frame 0; swell into Focus; ring out under logo
- Music cue guidance: lock major slams near strong cues ~16–23s when readable
- Audio-reactive treatment: expressive — mark/glow scale with bass on dark scenes
- SFX posture: denser — impact on every stinger, keys for typing, switch into Focus, logo hit
- Restraint rule: never bury readable text under chaos; slam then HOLD
