# Brag Plan: LifeOS (full product)

## What is this app?
LifeOS is a personal life operating system: one workspace for planning, school, work, money awareness, notes, calendar, and deep focus — an environment-based OS instead of a pile of apps.

## The angle
Show the whole OS in one breath: environments you can turn on, the always-on rails (Now · Tasks · Calendar · Library), School that actually tracks coursework, Focus that protects attention, and optional depth packs (Work, Study Abroad, Treasury, Mastery). Specific, calm, product-true — not a generic SaaS montage.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
Dark preauth: brand mark + **LifeOS** + serif **Your life, in focus.**

## Key moments (the middle)
- **Environments** — Life · School · Work · Study Abroad · Treasury · Mastery chips land one by one
- **Now capture** — `/t Finish reading chapter 3` types into the command bar; task row lands
- **School** — class cards (CS 240, HIST 110) + assignment due row + grade chip
- **Tasks + Calendar** — task list beside a week grid with real-looking events
- **Library** — note pages + Brain inbox thought
- **Focus** — dark session on the school task
- **Depth packs** — Work · Abroad · Treasury · Mastery as a final capability row

## Outro / punchline
Brand mark + **LifeOS** + **One OS. Every part of life.**

## User flow worth showing
1. Enter Now → capture with `/t`
2. See it live in School + Tasks/Calendar + Library
3. Drop into Focus; glimpse optional packs

## Tone
- Preset: polished (with denser app-store mid-section for coverage)
- Creative direction: full-product OS tour in one calm cut
- Interpretation: Soft crossfades, short settled labels, sequential chip/card reveals; no chaotic cuts

## Format: landscape — 1920x1080
## Duration: 25 seconds
Reason for the full 25s window: covering the complete LifeOS surface (core rails + School + Focus + optional packs) without flashing unreadably.

- Role: warm bed with sparse professional accents
- Music: `happy-beats-business-moves-vol-1-by-ende-dot-app.mp3` trimmed to 25s, volume ~0.18–0.2
- Music treatment: soft fade-in; fade under outro from ~22.5s
- Music cue guidance: bundled vol-1 cues; optional locks near env/chip reveals and Focus enter
- Audio-reactive treatment: subtle glow/warmth on dark scenes
- SFX posture: sparse — key ticks, soft drops for chips/cards, switch into Focus, soft logo hit
