# Brag Plan: LifeOS

## What is this app?
LifeOS is a calm personal operating system — tasks, school, notes, calendar, and deep focus in one synced workspace, with **Now** as the daily cockpit.

## The angle
Not another to-do list. An OS for deciding what matters *now*: type `/t`, drop into Focus, keep Life and School in the same graph. The video should feel like opening a quiet, confident product — paper + ink, serif moments, equalizer brand mark — not a purple SaaS explainer.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
Dark preauth moment: brand wordmark **LifeOS**, then the serif line **Your life, in focus.** lands hard and holds. Equalizer brand-mark bars pulse once like a soft equalizer kick.

## Key moments (the middle)
- **Now cockpit** — paper panel UI with a capture bar; cursor/typing simulates `/t Finish reading chapter 3` and Enter.
- **Task lands** — a real-looking task row appears in the Now queue (“Finish reading chapter 3 · School”).
- **Focus** — cut to the dark Focus mode: large serif task title, green “in session” dot, timer counting with calm confidence.

## Outro / punchline
Final hold: brand mark + **LifeOS** + short line **Now is your cockpit.** Soft fade. No fake metrics.

## User flow worth showing
1. **Entry** — Now view (daily cockpit).
2. **Key action** — Capture with `/t Finish reading chapter 3`.
3. **Result** — Task appears; Focus session opens on that task.

## Tone
- Preset: polished
- Creative direction: quiet premium product film for a personal OS
- Interpretation: Fewer scenes, longer holds, restrained motion, confidence through calm. Soft crossfades. Tasteful UI SFX only.

## Format: landscape — 1920x1080
## Duration: 20 seconds

## Visual identity (from the project)
- Background: `#F6F7F9` (canvas) / dark hero `#0a0a0c`–`#141418`
- Accent: `#625AF6` (light) / `#8B83FF` (dark moments)
- Text: `#202124` (ink) / `#F1F1F2` (dark)
- Display font: Iowan Old Style / Baskerville / Times (serif)
- Body font: Inter
- Strongest visual element: preauth serif hero + brand-mark (dark rounded square, three white bars) + Now capture bar + Focus mode dark stage

## Share copy (draft)
LifeOS — your life, in focus. Capture with `/t`, run Focus, keep school and life in one calm OS.

## Audio direction
- Role: warm bed with sparse professional accents
- Music: `happy-beats-business-moves-vol-1-by-ende-dot-app.mp3` (120 BPM, upbeat-but-usable; keep volume low/polished)
- Music treatment: start ~0s at low bed (~0.18–0.22), gentle fade-in 0.4s; fade under final logo from ~17.5s
- Music cue guidance: preset at `assets/music/cues/happy-beats-business-moves-vol-1-by-ende-dot-app.music-cues.*`; strong cues near 16.02 / 17.02 / 18.02 / 20.02 for Focus reveal + outro — optional within ±0.15s
- Audio-reactive treatment: subtle; soft brand-mark glow / Focus stage warmth with RMS — no waveforms
- SFX posture: sparse; motion-matched key ticks for `/t` typing, soft click on Enter, soft switch into Focus
- Audio-coupled moments: typed capture command; task row land; Focus enter; final logo settle
- Restraint rule: no dense UI spam; no chaotic glitch beds; never drown the serif hook

## Storyboard

### Scene 1 — Hook: Your life, in focus — 3.2s
Dark preauth shell. Wordmark LifeOS. Serif headline **Your life, in focus.** Brand-mark bars settle. Eyebrow “Welcome” optional and tiny.
Sequential/interaction: none (type is already settled; optional soft mark pulse)
Audio intent: quiet confidence; bed eases in
Audio-coupled idea: soft presence on mark
Music: warm bed under
Transition mood: soft → Scene 2

### Scene 2 — Reveal: Now is your cockpit — 3.0s
Light canvas. Recreate Now header + current-task card chrome. Line **Now is your cockpit.** as page eyebrow/title moment. Env chips Life / School tint briefly.
Sequential/interaction: yes — Life then School env chips appear
Audio intent: paper UI settle
Audio-coupled idea: two soft card/chip lands
Transition mood: soft → Scene 3

### Scene 3 — Capture: `/t` — 5.5s
Now capture bar focused. Text types `/t Finish reading chapter 3` character-by-character. Enter. Task row appears below.
Sequential/interaction: yes — typing then task row land
Audio intent: keyboard ticks + soft confirm
Audio-coupled idea: keypresses + drop on task row
Transition mood: soft → Scene 4

### Scene 4 — Focus — 5.0s
Dark Focus mode. Serif task title “Finish reading chapter 3”. Green in-session dot. Timer holds ~45:00 then ticks once for life. Align enter near strong cue ~16–17s if it doesn’t hurt readability.
Sequential/interaction: timer digit tick once
Audio intent: deeper bed presence; soft switch into scene
Audio-coupled idea: switch / soft impact on enter
Transition mood: soft → Scene 5

### Scene 5 — Outro — 3.3s
Centered brand mark + **LifeOS** + line **Decide what matters now.** Hold, fade music.
Sequential/interaction: none
Audio intent: fade bed; let logo settle
Audio-coupled idea: final soft logo hit
Transition mood: end

**Music mood for this video:** polished / warm upbeat bed (kept quiet)
**Audio summary:** Low warm bed under a calm product demo; sparse key/UI accents; soft fade into the logo hold.
